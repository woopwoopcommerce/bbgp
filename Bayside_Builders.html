What BII actually covers

Building Indemnity Insurance protects you if your builder becomes bankrupt, disappears or dies and cannot complete the work or fix defects covered by the five‑year statutory warranty. It applies to domestic building work costing $20,000 or more that requires council approval. Always ask for the certificate and verify it at bii.sa.gov.au before work begins.

08Common Questions

What Adelaide homeowners ask us first

In South Australia, BII is required for domestic building work costing $20,000 or more that requires council approval. If your project meets those thresholds, we take out the policy and provide you (and your council or private certifier) with a copy before work starts. You can verify the certificate at bii.sa.gov.au.
You can search our licence number on the Consumer and Business Services (CBS) public register, or call CBS on 131 882. We encourage every client to do this before signing a contract — it protects you and it keeps the industry honest.
We focus on Adelaide's coastal suburbs (Glenelg, Henley Beach, Grange, Brighton, Somerton Park), inner‑east and inner‑west suburbs (Unley, Norwood, Prospect, Walkerville), and selected hills locations. We also build in growth corridors such as Mawson Lakes. If you're unsure, send us your suburb and block details.
We provide fixed‑price contracts with a detailed scope and inclusions. That means the price you sign is the price you pay, unless you request a variation after signing. We do not rely on vague provisional sums that leave room for surprise increases.
